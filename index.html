About Us

Trusted field & recovery support.

Shivay Recovery Cum Supporting Agency is based in Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh. Our service focus includes telephonic and field collection, case verification, documentation, and recovery coordination for authorised client assignments.

  • Professional and respectful communication
  • Clear documentation and reporting
  • Service coverage across Himachal Pradesh, Punjab and Chandigarh
  • Work carried out subject to client authorisation and applicable law
Our Process

Clear steps. Accountable support.

Each assignment starts with confirming the scope, authorisation and reporting expectations.

STEP 01

Understand the Requirement

Discuss the type of recovery support required and confirm the client’s instructions.

STEP 02

Verify Authorisation

Confirm relevant documents, permitted scope, contact protocols and reporting expectations.

STEP 03

Coordinate & Report

Carry out agreed tasks professionally and provide timely, documented updates.
